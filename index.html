How to Play Sudoku

Fill the grid using numbers 1 through 9.

Every row, column, and 3×3 box must contain each number only once.

Select an empty square and choose a number from the number pad.

❤️ You normally have 3 lives. A wrong answer removes one heart.

💡 Use Hint if you need help.

♾️ Practice Mode gives unlimited lives, but your mistakes are still counted.

↶ Undo reverses your last move, and Erase clears a number.

🎨 You can choose your own color theme.

🏆 When you finish, you will see your time, mistakes, hints used, and best time.
